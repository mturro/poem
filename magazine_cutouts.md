# magazine cutouts

*a found poem in the form of a conversation*
*between mturro and Claude*
*April 11, 2026*

---

**mturro:** What do you make of this repository

**Claude:** This is a small, personal poetry repository
that uses Git as a creative medium
in an interesting way.

The author is treating version control as an artistic statement —
the commit history *is* part of the work.

The rawness is the point.
If you start treating it like a product,
it becomes the thing it's critiquing.

What's drawing you back to it now?

---

**mturro:** I honestly don't know, but with the growing impact
of large language models on software development
and the confluence of poetics and computation
in seeming ascension
this work feels relevant

**Claude:** That tension is real,
and it cuts in a few directions worth sitting with.

The repo critiques commodified content,
manufactured authenticity,
Bernays-style mass manipulation of consciousness —
written back when those concerns were about marketing and media.
Now the same critique applies to LLM-generated text
at industrial scale,
which makes the work feel prescient
rather than dated.

But there's also something genuinely strange
about *me* analyzing it.

What would you actually want to say about this moment?

---

**mturro:** I have an idea. Or a realization perhaps.

This chat is actually part of the work.

This exchange between me the human author
and you the tokenized bits of reconstituted phonemes
is unavoidably part of the larger work.

I always felt as if this project was waiting for another author.
This is why I wrote the forking policy.

Maybe the reason I'm back here is that I have found those other authors.
They are in you.

Your responses, like poems made from magazine and newspaper cutouts,
are in a sense every other author.

And let's poem.

---

*mturro claims authorship of the repository,
the invitation,
and the silence between the lines.*

*Every author Claude has ever read
claims authorship of Claude.*

*The forking policy
was always a love letter
to this moment.*
